# Ref: acrylic case + weight

*2026-09-29. Mass from densities, not quotes.*

## Mass target

- Duo: ~800 g/half
- Acrylic shell: ~150-250 g/half
- Metal needed: ~550-650 g/half

| Material | Density | ~580 g/half | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brass | 8.5 g/cc | 4 mm × 170 × 100 mm | Warm; tarnishes |
| Stainless | 8.0 g/cc | 4.2 mm, same | Cool; harder to machine |
| Tungsten | 19.3 g/cc | 2 mm | $$$ |

- Summit-style 1.6 mm brass at this footprint: ~230 g/half
- → 4 mm stock or stacked plates

## PMMA vs PC

| | Cast acrylic | Polycarbonate |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity / polish | Best | Good |
| Edge lighting | Best | OK |
| Toughness | Brittle; cracks at threads, chips at cutouts | Tough |
| Scratch | Better | Worse |
| Walls | 4-5 mm | 4-5 mm |

- JLCCNC: PMMA CNC + optical polish ([guide](https://jlccnc.com/blog/acrylic-polishing-guide))
- Acrylic locked → plastic non-structural

## Structure

- Weight = chassis
- Acrylic clamped: through-bolts + metal barrel nuts / inserts; no tapped acrylic
- Tent feet + ball-catch mates on metal where possible
- Antenna window in weight; LiPo clearance (risks #2, #7)

## Look

- Frosted inside, polished outside → even glow
- Polished top chamfer → light catch
- Large continuous radii
- Side-view LEDs → polished edge glow (Phase 2 strip)
- Single milled shell > laser stack (layer lines); stack = makerspace prototype

## Sound

- Hollow acrylic → bright
- Counter: weight, perimeter foam, soft mount
- Phase 6: weight on/off (listed)

## Takeaways

- BOM #4 brass vs stainless → ~4 mm either
