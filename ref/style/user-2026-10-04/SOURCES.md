## Related source material

`../modmusings-xray/INDEX.md` contains the downloaded full-size collection-page media and direct source URLs. Best likely matches for the keyboard images are the numbered Bauer Lite, Protagonist/Wired In, X-Ray HD, and R1 renders/photos; these are sibling assets from the official collection, not asserted as pixel-identical to every supplied crop. The Protagonist X-Ray edition is a Wired In 60% keyboard with polycarbonate top and bottom; the collection page lists 7° angle and 18.8 mm front height.
