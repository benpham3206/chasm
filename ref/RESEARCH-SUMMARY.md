## 3. Case-shape references

- **Mini Evo 75:** exact-name and variant searches found no verifiable keyboard maker/product, photos, dimensions, or profile drawing. See `shape/mini-evo-75/INDEX.md`.
- **Neo75:** identified as Neo Studio / Qwertykeys Neo75 family. Neo75 Cu reported at 318.3 × 138.8 mm, 16.8 mm front height and 7° typing angle; standard Neo75 has a conflicting 17.6 mm listing. The Cu variant's distinguishing side design is its curved reveal showing the contrasting bottom between top shell and battery compartment. No numerical curve radius or CAD/STEP was located. See `shape/neo75/INDEX.md`.

Best three shape references: official Neo75 product gallery top/product image, Neo75 Cu side profile from its official product-page/gallery imagery, and the published 16.8 mm / 7° dimensional specification summarized in the Neo75 index. A technical drawing or model remains a gap.
