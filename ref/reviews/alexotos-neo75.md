# alexotos — Neo75 (from "Neo75 and Neo98 review", Neo75 parts only)

Source: https://www.alexotos.com/neo75-and-neo98-review/ (read 2026-10-04 by Claude; Luna's saved page was empty). Images: `neo75-img/`.

| Topic | Notes |
|---|---|
| Mount | Three options: **top mount** ("well rounded sound profile", stiffer), **silicone gasket** ("fuller sound without feeling overly muted or mushy"), **PORON gasket** ("brighter and poppy", finicky to install) |
| Plate / PCB / foam | Plastic plate (aluminium option); "subtle" bottom foam; force-break pads between case pieces; hot-swap only PCB (no solder) |
| Sound / feel | "leans lower-pitched overall. Not muddy, not hollow, just slightly bass-forward, especially on the bottom row"; plastic plate "sound great here", aluminium "a bit brighter and snappier"; stabs "serviceable" but need tuning |
| Shape | "thin bezels, a refined top-down look, and a really strong side profile"; front height ~18 mm, "very comfortable for longer sessions"; stainless accent badge |
| Criticisms | fixed layout / no solder PCB; hidden screws complicate disassembly; software minor |

## Takeaways for chasm (mini Evo75 halves, Neo75 lines)

- Silhouette: Evo75 box-over-wedge with a graduated side curve (`../shape/evo75/SUMMARY.md`); keep Neo75-style thin, even bezels and a clean top-down outline.
- Front height target 18–20 mm (Neo75 ~18, Evo75 19.3), typing angle 7–8°.
- Typing feel: Evo75 butterfly leaf springs + PP plate + 1.2 mm flex-cut PCB; silicone gaskets give the "fuller, not mushy" Neo75 character.
- Small stainless accent (our gull) fits the Neo75 badge idea.
- Keep the internals serviceable (ball catch rather than hidden screws).
