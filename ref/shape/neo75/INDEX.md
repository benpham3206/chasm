## Dimensions and profile notes

- Neo75 Cu reported case footprint: **318.3 × 138.8 mm**; front height **16.8 mm**; fixed typing angle **7°**. The dimensions/angle appear in the launch specifications and independent review/search extracts; they are not in a downloadable dimension drawing retrieved here.
- A product reseller page reports 17.6 mm front height for the standard Neo75. This differs by variant/source; do not assume it applies to Neo75 Cu.
- Neo75 Cu uses a three-layer visual side profile: top case, exposed bottom case (copper or other finish), and battery compartment/cover. The side incorporates a curved facet/reveal between top case and battery compartment, showing the contrasting bottom material along the perimeter. The upper shell has a concave/downward curved machining line paired with a corresponding battery-cover curve. Front and side faces show the contrast band; the rear rounds into the sculpted bottom shape.
- No numerical radius, section drawing, STEP, or CAD source was found. Curve shape is qualitative only; the available images are reference, not dimensionally authoritative.
